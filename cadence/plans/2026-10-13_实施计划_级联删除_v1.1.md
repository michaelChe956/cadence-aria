### 0.2 拟新增接口字典（统一、不是已有API）

以下公共类型/API放D1.0新增模块并由后包消费，现存根路径取 `ProductAppPaths.issue_lifecycle_root/issue_root`，不得自己全仓glob：

- `DeletionEntityKind::{Story,Design,Plan}`；`EntityScope { project_id:String,issue_id:String,kind:DeletionEntityKind,entity_id:String }`。
- `EntityDeletionRecord { scope:EntityScope,phase:DeletionPhase,started_at:String,deleted_at:Option<String> }`；`DeletionPhase::{Purging,Deleted}`。记录仅一条 scope/kind/id/phase/time，禁止内容/hash/ref/manifest。路径 `entity-deletions/{kind}/{id}.json` 在 lifecycle root。
- `EntityDeletionStore::{new(ProductAppPaths),get(&EntityScope)->Result<Option<EntityDeletionRecord>,ProductStoreError>,begin(&EntityDeletionLease)->Result<EntityDeletionRecord,ProductStoreError>,complete(&EntityDeletionLease)->Result<EntityDeletionRecord,ProductStoreError>}`；begin/complete同条幂等更新，完成时间不被重试覆盖。
- `EntityOperationStore::{new(ProductAppPaths),acquire_run(&EntityScope)->Result<EntityRunLease,EntityOperationError>,try_acquire_delete(&EntityScope)->Result<EntityDeletionLease,EntityOperationError>,with_live_write<T>(&EntityScope,impl FnOnce()->Result<T,ProductStoreError>)->Result<T,ProductStoreError>}`。run共享准入在最外层取得；delete为try-exclusive。**with_live_write必须try-shared，锁争用立即返回Deleting，再映射ProductStoreError::Conflict{kind:"entity_deleting",id}，禁止阻塞等待scope**；持lease内私有helper不重取scope。`EntityOperationError::{Busy,Deleting,Deleted,Store(ProductStoreError)}`，稳定inode不随purge unlink，锁内读墓碑，非安全/确认授权。
- `with_issue_relations<T>(project_id:&str,issue_id:&str,operation:impl FnOnce()->Result<T,ProductStoreError>)->Result<T,ProductStoreError>`。固定序scope集合（包含引用上游，外层排序）→稳定repo resource锁→关系短锁→session/object。**持session/engine Mutex或任何对象flock期间不得首次取scope锁**；内层消费已有EntityRunLease或私有已准入helper。嵌套取scope即使try也不当合法设计；真实接线依据§0.4。run不持关系锁，delete只try独占。
- `EntityDeletionManifest {scope:EntityScope,session_ids:Vec<String>,work_item_ids:Vec<String>,review_ids:Vec<String>,provider_run_ids:Vec<String>,verification_plan_ids:Vec<String>,repository_profile_ids:Vec<String>,target_repository_ids:Vec<String>,advance_ids:Vec<String>,advance_retry_command_ids:Vec<String>}`只真实ID，无内容/digest。`LifecycleStore::collect_entity_deletion_manifest(&EntityScope)->Result<EntityDeletionManifest,ProductStoreError>`、`list_incoming_entity_references(&EntityScope)->Result<Vec<EntityReference>,ProductStoreError>`；`EntityReference{kind:String,id:String}`。D1.0的EntityDeletionStore产`save_pending_manifest(&EntityDeletionLease,&EntityDeletionManifest)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`、`load_pending_manifest(&EntityDeletionLease)->Result<Option<EntityDeletionManifest>,ProductStoreError>`、`remove_pending_manifest(&EntityDeletionLease)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`；purge前落pending-manifest.json，complete前删。根缺失反查接口见§0.5，不依赖v1.0另读签名。
- `WorkItemPlanSourceStore::purge_plan_sources(project_id:&str,issue_id:&str,plan_id:&str)->Result<(),SourceStoreError>`；低层只清source plan子树，不包含HTTP/manager逻辑。
- `LifecycleStore::purge_entity_owned_children(&EntityDeletionLease,&EntityDeletionManifest)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`、`delete_entity_root(&EntityDeletionLease)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`；成功时子依赖先于持ref拥有记录删除。旧web实体delete caller迁到共用协调顺序；低层旧delete接口只作为独占清理实现或移除，不留公开旁路。纯ID pending manifest仅用于重入，成功删除，不留永久第二记录。
- `DeletionConfirmation::{NotRequired,Required{reason:DeletionConfirmationReason}}`，reason=`PreviouslyConfirmed|LegacyHistoryUnknown`；`classify_entity_deletion_confirmation(&EntityScope)->Result<DeletionConfirmation,ProductStoreError>`。三类record拟增`ever_confirmed:Option<bool>`；新创建Some(false)，任何确凿确认/发布成功根写Some(true)，回退/替换/restore取单调合并，不改CAS。
- HTTP现DELETE拟加 `DeleteEntityQuery {confirmed:Option<bool>}`，成功仍 `{status:"deleted"}`；409 stable code `deletion_confirmation_required|entity_referenced|entity_busy`，既有`coding_workspace_exists`原details保留；不把query布尔当安全授权。
- 既有前端三个delete函数拟增第4参 `options?:{confirmed?:boolean}`，默认URL无query；仅同意弹窗后传true。服务端分类通过ApiRequestError code/details回传，不暴露ever_confirmed给前端做权威判断，避免扩展无关DTO。
- 身份准入（D1.0拟增、D1.3a消费）：`reserve_entity_identity(&EntityScope)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`（同存活scope幂等、tombstone拒复用）、`allocate_workspace_session_id()->Result<String,ProductStoreError>`（现全项目session最大序号与耐久高水位取max再增）、`bind_workspace_session_identity(session_id:&str,&EntityScope)->Result<(),ProductStoreError>`（只ID/scope、同身份幂等）、`scope_for_workspace_session(session_id:&str)->Result<EntityScope,ProductStoreError>`（missing/歧义/墓碑fail-closed）。旧记录在本实体删除前迁移身份索引，不把删过session ID重新分给新实体。
- shared repo资源（D1.0拟增、D1.3c/D2.2消费）：`SharedResourceKey {project_id:String,issue_id:String,repository_id:Option<LogicalRepositoryId>}`（None仅真实Legacy issue shared路径）。`try_acquire_shared_resource_write(&SharedResourceKey)->Result<SharedResourceWriteLease,EntityOperationError>`与`try_acquire_shared_resource_cleanup(&SharedResourceKey)->Result<SharedResourceCleanupLease,EntityOperationError>`，均非阻塞独占短lease，inode在purge外；resource争用Busy，不持旧object锁再取。scope能证则先scope lease，未知资源归属不能按target猜Plan，安全规则见D1.3c。
